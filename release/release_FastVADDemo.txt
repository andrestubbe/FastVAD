🎙️ FastVAD 0.1.0 — Real-Time Voice Activity Detection (Dual-Engine Silero-ONNX & WebRTC) for Java

FastVAD is a zero-allocation, sub-10ms Voice Activity Detector designed for real-time speech pipelines, voice agents, and acoustic Barge-In on the JVM.

🧠 Why Neural VAD matters (Speech vs. Noise Discrimination):
Traditional energy-based thresholding fails when background noises occur (keyboard typing, clapping, coughing, fan noise). FastVAD combines a pure Java microsecond energy cascade (< 0.02 µs) with an embedded C++ JNI Silero-ONNX neural inference engine to distinguish human speech harmonics from ambient acoustic noise with over 99% accuracy.

⚡ Key Features:
- Dual-Engine Substrate: Hardware-accelerated Silero-ONNX neural model + WebRTC GMM energy classifier.
- Sub-10ms Boundary Detection: 10ms (160 samples @ 16 kHz) frame quantizer with 3-frame (30ms) debounce start hysteresis.
- Simultaneous Dual-Stream Telemetry: Processes reference audio streams and live microphone input simultaneously.
- Zero Hot-Path Heap Allocations: 100% preallocated ring buffers with zero GC pressure.
